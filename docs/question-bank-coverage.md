# Question-bank coverage

Updated for the final pre-pilot gate on 2026-08-07.

## Inventory and review status

- 66 structurally valid, versioned items across six categories.
- 48 pilot-eligible items: `approved`, active, and publishable.
- 16 legacy items are `needs_review` and cannot enter an assessment. Six need distractor/roadmap review; ten need difficulty/overlap review.
- 2 authoritative exemplars are `do_not_publish`: `SKM-AI-HARD-001` (matching) and `SKM-PS-HARD-001` (ranking). Their content remains unchanged, but the dedicated interactions are not pilot-ready.
- Each category retains exactly enough eligible material for a form with 3 easy, 3 medium, and 2 hard items.

## Quality-gate decision

The 32 approved legacy items were retained only where their answer is unambiguous, vocabulary is appropriate for the target grades, the Uzbek and English meanings align, and the item supports an actionable competency. The remaining 16 were conservatively withheld; they were not automatically called approved.

The approved methodology exemplars were not rewritten. Sixteen enter the live pool. Matching and ranking remain visible in admin with `do_not_publish` status and are rejected by both assembly and validation.

## Coverage limitations

The methodology does not provide the proposed 180-item operational bank. The current pool supports one valid 48-item content set with deterministic presentation order, but limited alternate-form variation. Several competencies have only one item, so the results page reports “insufficient coverage” rather than a precise competency label when fewer than two responses support it.

Before a larger or comparative pilot, an educator should review the 16 held items, add independently reviewed replacements, run bilingual cognitive interviews, and conduct item-level psychometric analysis on pilot responses.
